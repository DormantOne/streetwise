    Package a run

    On the laptop at the bench, by the printer

    A run is one batch: the same medicine, from one lot, split into identical bags that all go into the same box.

    1. Box and count. Which box the bags go into, and how many bags you're making, up to 60.
    2. Medication. Name, strength or form, and how many go in each bag.
    3. Lot and expiry, copied from the stock bottle. Expiry is year and month, like 2027-09. The page shows how it read what you typed.
    4. SIG options. The directions the physician can choose from, up to three. Write the daily limit inside each one.
    5. Press Package. The bag numbers appear, and every field clears for the next run.

    The Package button is a helpful nag. Until everything's filled in, it says how many things are missing, and the line under it says which.

    Nothing carries over between runs, on purpose. A lot number left over from the last medicine is the mistake that matters, so always copy it fresh from the bottle in your hand.

    Two double-checks. An expired lot, or more than 20 in one bag, makes the site ask you to confirm before it packages anything.

    Bag note is for the team only. It doesn't print anywhere.

      Print the stickers

      On the laptop at the bench, by the printer

      One sticker per bag, eighteen to a sheet of label paper. The code on each one is how that bag gets logged later.

      1. On the done screen, press Print stickers. (Later, you'll also find it on any run on the Stock page.)
      2. Load label paper, then press Print.
      3. In the print box, choose 100% or actual size. Not "fit to page", which shrinks the codes until phones misread them.
      4. Once the sheet is actually out of the printer, press Mark printed.
      5. Stick one sticker on the outside of each bag.

      Mark printed is a promise that the stickers exist. Until you press it, these bags count as no sticker on the Stock page. Afterwards they count as in box.

      Jammed, or came out wrong? On the Stock page, Not printed after all takes it back within 24 hours, as long as nobody has handed one out.

      Two print jobs, two kinds of paper. Right after you press Print, the page reminds you to swap to plain paper for the prescription sheets.

        Print the script and the receipt

        On the laptop at the bench, by the printer

        Print prescriptions gives each bag one sheet with two halves: the script, for the person, and the receipt, for the team. Both are attached to the bag, blank, until it’s handed out.

        1. Press Print prescriptions on the done screen, or on the sticker sheet. You get one landscape sheet per bag.
        2. Print at 100% with margins set to none, so the cut line lands in the middle.
        3. Cut down the dashed line. The left half is the receipt (it says Physician copy). The right half is the script.
        4. Fold the script at FOLD HERE, printed sides out, so the help numbers end up on the back, the right way up.
        5. Attach the script and the receipt to the bag, with its sticker. Leave them blank: they’re filled out at the handout.

        Nothing is filled in at the bench. The script and the receipt travel with the bag and are completed when it’s handed out (stop 5).

        "Scaled to fit" is a cue to check. Long or multiple directions can crowd the card, so the page shrinks it and tells you. Make sure it's still easy to read on paper before anyone signs.

        These don't change the stock count. Only Mark printed, on the sticker sheet, does that.

        The help numbers live on the back of the script only. That's deliberate, so there's never a second list going out of date. You won't find them on the site, or on this page.

          Hand out a bag

          On your phone, out on the street

          Scan the sticker, fill out the script, keep the receipt, and log it. On the phone, after the first time, it's one tap.

          1. Point your phone's camera at the sticker and open the link it finds. The page shows what's in the bag; check there's no red warning.

            Take back a handout

            On your phone

            Scanned the wrong bag, or the person changed their mind? How you undo it depends on how long ago you logged it.

            1. Scan any bag. At the bottom of its page is What you have logged: everything from this phone.
            2. Within the first hour, your own log has Undo. Nothing to type.
            3. From one hour up to 24, it says Undo (code). Type the team password or the admin password.
            4. After 24 hours, nobody can undo it. The bag is with a person now, and the record stands. If it's wrong, add a comment.

            Undoing keeps the record of the mistake. The bag goes back in its box and can go to somebody else.

            Someone else's log? Ask them to undo it from their phone. If the bag is actually back in a box, the next box count can set it right with It is here… (stop 8).

              Read the stock page

              On the laptop

              A laptop job. Inventory shows every bag the team has packaged, and where each one is now.

              1. On the laptop, click Inventory on the home screen, or at the top of the Package page.
              2. Read the numbers on top: total bags, dispensed (handed out), in boxes, and no sticker.
              3. Switch views with the five buttons under them: Runs Boxes Bags Lots Reconcile box.

              The numbers always add up. In boxes + no sticker + dispensed = total bags. If they ever don't, a red line says so. That means the data needs a look, so tell whoever runs the site.

              Runs is every batch, newest first. A red NOT PRINTED means nothing in that run can be scanned yet. Boxes is the same runs, grouped by box.

              Bags is one row per bag: search, filter, tick a few to move them together, or open one to read its whole history. Download CSV gives exactly the rows you're looking at.

              Lots is one row per medicine and lot, which is what a recall applies to.

                Count a box

                On the laptop, with the box open beside it

                Boxes drift. Every so often, someone brings one to the laptop, opens the lid, and checks it against the record, bag by bag.

                1. On the stock page, choose Reconcile box. Boxes never counted are at the top, then the ones counted longest ago.
                2. Click Open this box for a checklist of every bag the record puts in it.
                3. Tick each bag you can see. All there? Tick all accounted for does the lot.
                4. Something off? Click open on that bag: Not present, Removed just now, Needs sig card, Sig card is there, Move to box…, Comment… or History.
                5. Press Save count. Nothing is written until you do.

                ★ means look closer: the lot is pulled or flagged, it's out of date or expiring, it has no sig card, or it was handed out and taken back.

                Handed out from this box lists bags that are with people. They're why a box of forty holds thirty-six. Nothing to tick.

                Holding one of those? Click It is here… and saving the count puts it back in stock. It's the one fix with no time limit, because the bag in your hand is the proof.

                Found a bag that isn't on the list? Type its number under Found a bag that is not on this list and press Check before adding it.

                  Fix a mistake

                  On the laptop

                  Nothing in pillbag is ever deleted. Every fix keeps who made it, when and why, so pick the one that matches what actually happened.

                  1. Say what happened, using the buttons beside this text.
                  2. It shows the exact buttons to click, in order, on the stock page on the laptop.
                  3. Give a reason when you're asked. It's kept alongside the change for good.

                  Flag and Pull are different acts. Flag marks a lot to look at again and takes nothing out of stock. Pull is the recall.

                  If a fix asks for the admin password, that's a deliberate extra check your team can switch on. You stay signed in as yourself, it lasts 30 minutes, and drop admin at the top gives it back early.

                  Comments never hold patient details. Say what was wrong with the record, not who the person was.

                    "This code didn't come up."
                    The scan may have cut off the link, or the bag isn't in the system. Scan again, closer. Still nothing? Ask the team.
